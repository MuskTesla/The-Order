The data keeps that license, separate from The Order's application license.

The Order bundles the complete published dist/data.json files in lossless gzip
form, with SHA-256 and immutable Git revisions in snapshot-manifest.json.

or video files are bundled. Fork season_id fields only supplement compatible
main-dataset rows. Linking a platform is not a guarantee of current rights,
availability, region access, or endorsement by the data contributors.
